L&T Named India’s Most Sustainable Infrastructure Company
Prestigious financial publication
Business Today
has named L&T India’s Most Sustainable Company in Infrastructure. The coveted honour was conferred on L&T at BT’s ‘India’s Most Sustainable Companies Summit & Awards 2026’ ceremony hosted in New Delhi on 6 June. Union Minister of Environment, Forest and Climate Change Bhupender Yadav handed over the award to Santosh Kumar Singh, Head – Sustainability, L&T.
Mr. S. N. Subrahmanyan Conferred Business Today’s  Best CEO Urban Visionary Award
In recognition of his visionary leadership and outstanding contribution to the industry, L&T CMD S N Subrahmanyan (SNS) has been conferred the Best CEO - Urban Visionary Award in the Infrastructure segment by Business Today. The recipients of the BT Awards are determined by several factors – including financial performance, future-readiness in an increasingly competitive market, strategic execution and the ability to build a robust business model.
